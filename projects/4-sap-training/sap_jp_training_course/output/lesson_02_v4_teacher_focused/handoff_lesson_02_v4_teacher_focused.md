## 2. 讲师明天如何上课

1. 先打开 `00_START_HERE_FOR_TEACHER.md`，确认本课主线。
2. 上课时打开：
   - `02_student_materials/01_student_ppt_outline_final.md`
   - `01_teacher_core/01_teacher_full_script_slide_by_slide.md`
